Welcome to HelloCuff. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, use our NFC wearables, or manage your HelloCuff digital profile (collectively, the "Service"). Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the Service.

**1. Information We Collect**
We collect information that identifies, relates to, or could reasonably be linked to you ("Personal Information") in the following ways:

* **Information You Provide to Us:**
* **Account Information:** When you register or purchase a HelloCuff subscription, we collect your name, email address, billing address, and payment information (processed securely by our third-party payment processors).
* **Profile Data:** Any information you choose to add to your HelloCuff digital profile, including contact details, social media links, photos, logos, and custom text.
* **Communications:** Information you provide when you contact us for customer support.


* **Information Collected Automatically:**
* **Usage and Device Data:** When you access our platform, we automatically collect your IP address, browser type, operating system, and data regarding your interaction with our website.
* **NFC Tap Analytics:** We may collect aggregate, anonymous data on how often your HelloCuff wearable is tapped or your profile is viewed to provide you with usage analytics.



**2. How We Use Your Information**
We use the information we collect to:

* Provide, operate, and maintain the HelloCuff Service.
* Process your transactions and manage your subscription.
* Display your chosen public profile data when your HelloCuff wearable is scanned.
* Improve, personalize, and expand our website and hardware functionality.
* Communicate with you, including sending technical notices, updates, security alerts, and support messages.
* Monitor and analyze trends, usage, and activities to improve user experience.
* Detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent transactions and other illegal activities.

**3. How We Share Your Information**
We do not sell your personal information. We only share your information in the following situations:

* **Publicly via Your Wearable:** The core function of HelloCuff is to share information. Any data you add to your public digital profile will be visible to anyone who physically taps your HelloCuff wearable or scans your link.
* **Service Providers:** We share information with third-party vendors who perform services for us, such as payment processing (e.g., Stripe), web hosting, and data analytics. These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data.
* **Legal Obligations:** We may disclose your information where required by law, subpoena, or other legal processes, or to protect the rights, property, and safety of HelloCuff, our users, or others.
* **Business Transfers:** In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as a business asset.

**4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies**
We use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to track activity on our website and hold certain information. Cookies help us understand how you use our site, remember your preferences, and improve your overall experience. You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent; however, some features of the Service may not function properly without them.

**5. Data Security and Retention**
We implement commercially reasonable security measures to protect your Personal Information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We retain your profile data and account information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you the Service, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

**6. Your Privacy Choices**

* **Managing Your Profile:** You can edit, update, or remove the information on your public HelloCuff profile at any time through your account dashboard.
* **Account Deletion:** You may request the deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us or using the deletion tool in your dashboard. Note that we cannot recall profile data that third parties have already downloaded or saved from a previous tap.
* **Opting Out of Communications:** You may opt out of receiving promotional emails by following the unsubscribe instructions included in those emails.

**7. Children’s Privacy**
HelloCuff is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete such information.
